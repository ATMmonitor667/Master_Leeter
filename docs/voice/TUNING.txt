VOICE LATENCY TUNING

Run `pnpm review -- <session-id>` after each human interview. Read response
latency p50/p95 together with material interruptions and missed answers. The
review TSV records responseLatencyMs, speechSource, classifierSource,
prosodyProbability, prosodyConfidence, prosodyPull, and largestStage.

If an interruption occurs, inspect the preceding transcript and prosody cue.
For a false confident endpoint, increase the confident minimum for that mode
by 100 ms or reduce PROSODY_WEIGHTS.sharpness. If prosody is absent and the
system still interrupts, treat it as a turn/gate defect and reproduce it in
simulation before tuning.

If answers are slow, inspect largestStage. Quiet onset to final transcript is
provider transcription and VAD delay. Final transcript to authorization is
classification or the silence hold; check classifierSource and prosodyPull.
Authorization to first sample is cache coverage, network, and playback. A
missed clear question is always a defect, not a tuning target.

Change one setting at a time, then record a new session. Keep
NEXT_PUBLIC_VAD_HANGOVER_MS=350: the six-utterance TTS probe found no accuracy
gain from 500 ms and a slower quiet-onset-to-final path. Confirm in human runs.
`NEXT_PUBLIC_TRANSCRIPT_COALESCE=off` restores chunk-by-chunk forwarding.
`TURN_END_PROSODY=off` restores clock-only policy timing;
`TTS_PRERENDER=off` restores realtime model speech; `NEXT_PUBLIC_ROOM_TONE=off`
removes the acoustic bed.
