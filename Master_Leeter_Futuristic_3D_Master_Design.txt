MANDATORY PRODUCT RULES — DESIGN MUST NEVER BREAK:
1. The full interview problem is spoken orally. Do not add visible prompt text.
2. No talking avatar, face, anthropomorphic character or AI chat transcript.
3. No constant coaching, glowing distractions, gamified interruptions or hints.
4. The live AI never grades. No live score, confidence meter or hidden-gate state.
5. No AI autocomplete or answer suggestions in the codepad.
6. Do not change the server-owned 45-minute timer or speech-authority gate.
7. Only actual provider playback should trigger an 'AI speaking' effect.
8. A barge-in must cancel playback and speaking animation IMMEDIATELY.
9. The dashboard must only show measured data, with honest unknown/empty states.
10. Preserve captions, keyboard navigation, reduced motion, contrast, consent,
    privacy/deletion, and reconnect/pending-save behavior.
11. No new audio recording or raw waveform retention to implement visuals.
12. Do not alter engine, session policy, evaluation, or persistence semantics
    as part of a visual redesign.

VISUAL THESIS
-------------
'An intelligent room made of light, glass and code.'

The same product has three INTENTIONAL intensities:
- LANDING = CINEMATIC. Dramatic, immersive, interactive 3D; emotional first
  impression; guided narrative and strong CTAs.
- DASHBOARD = PRECISE. Futuristic software instrument panel: spatial depth,
  softly luminous cards, intelligent hierarchy, mild optional 3D accent.
- LIVE INTERVIEW = QUIET. A premium black-box coding environment where the
  editor dominates; only useful status responds to the interview.

Avoid generic 'cyberpunk dashboard': no rain of green code, full-screen neon,
spinning holograms behind code, fake terminal diagnostics, blinking red timer,
or overly translucent glass that destroys legibility. Think sci-fi laboratory,
not casino or hacker movie. The feeling should be impressive BEFORE an interview
and almost invisible DURING one.

===============================================================
01 / RESEARCHED REFERENCES: WHAT TO STUDY AND WHAT TO ADAPT
===============================================================

REFERENCE A: LUSION — https://lusion.co/
Visual lesson:
- Interactive monochrome/blue sculpture floating in a dark studio volume.
- Strong contrast between quiet editorial typography and moving 3D object.
- Glossy hard-surface geometry; white diffuse highlights, dark cavities,
  near-black contact shadows, electric blue materials.
- Lusion v3 showcase uses an electric-blue motif around #1A2FFB alongside
  off-white and charcoal; color example is reference, not our exact palette.
- Important: restrained UI stays readable above complex moving art.
How to adapt:
- Hero 'Cognition Core': an abstract suspended glass/metal gyroscope made
  of four open rings, glowing signal traces and a soft inner luminous volume.
- Pointer-parallax affects sculpture only; it must never shift CTA positions.
- On scroll, rings align into one simple outline of an interview room.
Do not copy Lusion's shape arrangement, logo, original asset or animation.
Study: https://www.commarts.com/project/36283/lusion

REFERENCE B: IGLOO INC — https://www.igloo.inc/
Visual lesson:
- The whole viewport behaves as one cinematic place; tactile textures,
  volumetric-feeling light, fog and space create scale without many UI colors.
- Tiny technical annotations are secondary to the sculptural centerpiece.
- Near-monochrome surfaces emphasize differences in roughness and reflection.
How to adapt:
- Design 3D interview chamber as a spatial scene: dark architectural floor,
  horizon fog, thin concentric traces, one glowing suspended 'AI signal'.
- Use atmospheric depth sparingly and progressive enhancement for fog.
- Retain HTML text and DOM controls rather than rendering critical UI in WebGL.
Study: https://www.awwwards.com/sites/igloo-inc

REFERENCE C: NOOMO LABS — https://labs.noomoagency.com/
Study: https://noomoagency.com/insights/noomo-labs-immersive-3d-website
Visual lesson:
- Translucent overlapping materials, fluid motion, organic silhouette,
  lighting that produces glasslike volume, user-customizable appearance.
- The studio's own write-up explains its multiple meshes/materials, glass
  experiments, and customization knobs; these are documented methods.
How to adapt:
- AI presence is a SMALL ABSTRACT SIGNAL ORB, never a face or creature.
- Refract very subtly during noncritical marketing scenes; while coding,
  use a plain CSS/SVG disk with low-frequency, state-accurate motion.
- Limit glass distortions to art, not text, Monaco, or assistive labels.

REFERENCE D: ACTIVE THEORY — https://activetheory.net/
Visual lesson:
- Black-on-black environments become legible through deliberate pools of
  light, restrained particle rings and cinematic camera framing.
- Movement creates emotional weight; it does not require flashing everything.
How to adapt:
- Pin a single 3D moment as the landing's emotional peak.
- Use 2-3 deliberate reveal beats; not a perpetual scroll amusement ride.
- Keep the dashboard utilitarian and measured, even if landing is spectacular.
Study: https://www.awwwards.com/sites/active-theory-v5

REFERENCE E: BRUNO SIMON — https://bruno-simon.com/
Visual lesson:
- A real-time scene can make navigation memorable if the controls are clear.
How to adapt:
- Borrow playfulness for the landing 'explore demo' moment only.
- DO NOT turn the high-stakes interview into a game or require 3D navigation.
Study: https://www.awwwards.com/bruno-simon-portfolio-wins-site-of-the-month-november.html

RESEARCH SYNTHESIS — 5 RULES:
1. The most advanced visual systems use one unforgettable central object.
2. Light, material, and camera direction do more than arbitrary neon effects.
3. Motion is most powerful when tied to user intent and scene storytelling.
4. 3D and UX must be separable: progressive enhancement is not optional.
5. A memorable 3D marketing site and a good editor need opposite attention
   budgets. Give them separate render policies and separate bundles.

REFERENCE DECISION MATRIX:
- Lusion -> hero sculpture, framing, cursor inertia: ADOPT.
- Igloo -> environmental depth and atmospheric staging: ADOPT LIGHTLY.
- Noomo -> layered glass + sound-reactive organic energy: ADAPT AS ABSTRACT ORB.
- Active Theory -> filmic pacing, particle system: ADOPT LIGHTLY.
- Bruno Simon -> immersive exploration: LANDING-ONLY, OPTIONAL.

2.1 SHAPE GRAMMAR
-----------------
Primary primitives:
- A single bright 3D 'Cognition Core' made from 3 thin nested elliptical
  toroidal rings, central blurred pearl/field, and tiny orbital nodes.
- Ring widths: 0.045-0.09 world units, radius relationships 1.00 / 0.72 / 0.44.
- Low-poly sphere? NO. Orb is sculptural, not a generic glowing basketball.
- Outer rings tilt on different axes: 18deg, -27deg, 61deg initial values.
- Primary panels: 14px radius, not fully pill-shaped.
- Compact controls: 10px radius; 6px radius for terminal/tool tabs.
- Main buttons: 12px radius, minimum 44px height desktop, 48px touch.
- Status chips: 999px radius; small dot + explicit readable state text.
- Diagram lines: 1px; illuminated emphasis lines 1.5px max.
- One purposeful angled 'signal cut' MAY appear on marketing dividers.
- Do not apply chamfers to every card. Angular science-fiction flourishes
  should be accents, not a wholesale replacement for readable UI.

2.2 MATERIAL GRAMMAR
--------------------
Material 1 / OBSIDIAN METAL (hero structural rings):
  base #131B2E; metalness .72; roughness .25; opacity 1.
  edge emissive tint #5479FF at .16; soft directional reflections.
Material 2 / ION GLASS (hero outer lens):
  base tint #79CFFF; transmission .68; roughness .12; IOR 1.22;
  thickness .30; transparent material; no expensive full scene refraction
  where simpler fake Fresnel rim passes visual test.
Material 3 / PORCELAIN LIGHT (inner form):
  soft pearlescent #C8DDFB; metalness .08; roughness .42.
Material 4 / UI MATTE GLASS:
  opaque or near-opaque surface; NO blur under paragraphs or code;
  subtle border + top highlight + large shadow instead.
Material 5 / HAIRLINE CIRCUIT:
  1px #26334C line at 0.65 alpha; thin, sparse, decorative only.

2.3 LIGHTING
------------
- Key area light: upper-left, cool #D7E6FF, intensity relative 1.0.
- Rim light: rear-right violet #7A6AFF, intensity .6.
- Cyan fill: front low #4FDBFF, intensity .25.
- Floor: nearly black, not mirror-like; optional faint ground contact shadow.
- Add emissive detail to geometry; DO NOT rely on heavy full-screen bloom.
- Specular highlights: elongated and soft, never hard dazzling hot pixels.
- Allow shadow direction to explain each object in 3D.

03 / SIGNATURE 3D COMPONENT: THE COGNITION CORE
===============================================================

3.1 ANATOMY
-----------
- Purpose: a symbolic 'thinking chamber', not a person, brain, eye, or bot.
- 3 open concentric torus/arc elements, with discontinuities at independent
  azimuths (e.g., 24deg, 196deg, 311deg).
- Inner faint volume rendered as a low-resolution refractive-looking pearl.
- 5-9 tiny spherical 'signal nodes' orbit tracks; not random glitter.
- 2 soft circular wave surfaces represent exchanged speaking turns on the
  MARKETING page only.
- Overall model about 3.4 world units wide; centered at x=0, y=0.
- Perspective camera fov 35, z=9; frame centered with 15% breathing room.
- Material palette: dark chrome, translucent ice, indigo rim, cyan point light.
- Silhouette clear when rendered as still image at 320px wide.

3.2 MOTION
----------
- Idle: ring yaw moves a TOTAL of 8deg over ~20s, then returns; very slow.
- Parallax: cursor target normalized [-1,1] => yaw +/-6deg; pitch +/-4deg,
  interpolate with damping ~0.06/frame-equivalent. On touch no cursor parallax.
- Hover: if pointer over hero artwork and motion allowed, nearby orbit traces
  brighten from 12% to 35% opacity over 300ms; no large scale jumps.
- On scroll section progression: camera moves <=1.2 world units over scene;
  rings align from three orientations to an orderly plane, signaling clarity.
- Reduced motion: static still hero, no camera movement, no pointer motion.
- Losing visibility: animation loop stops while tab hidden.

3.3 SHADER DETAILS (OPTIONAL, ONLY IF BASIC VERSION PASSES)
------------------------------------------------------------
- Fresnel rim = pow(1 - dot(normal, viewDirection), 3.0), multiplied by subtle
  cyan/indigo emission. Clamp emissive response to avoid white clipping.
- Internal pearl = base cool diffuse + low-amplitude moving noise displacement;
  noise amplitude <= 0.025 world units, frequency <=0.25 Hz.
- Emission intensity bounded: not an HDR strobe. No phase-based flashes.
- Transparent objects sorted; if artifacts occur, replace costly multi-layer
  transmission with flat tinted transparent shell + Fresnel rim.
- Color pipeline: renderer sRGB output; consistent tone mapping; test color
  against CSS tokens. Avoid double gamma correction.

3.4 PERFORMANCE LEVELS
----------------------
Tier A / capable desktop: scene + simple lights + limited particles.
Tier B / less capable mobile: lower geometry segments, DPR cap 1.0, no blur,
  no transmission, particle count <=20.
Tier C / WebGL unavailable, prefers-reduced-motion, data-saver, battery
  constrained or user choice: pre-rendered AVIF/WebP hero still and CSS accent.
Never punish users with a blank hero when WebGL fails.

3.5 ASSET EXPORT
----------------
- Blender source: hero_core.blend (artist-owned original asset).
- Export optimized hero_core.glb and static hero_core.webp + hero_core.avif.
- GLB target <=350 KiB gzip/brotli for this geometric composition. Aim lower.
- Compression optional: Draco/Meshopt only if decoding cost is justified.
- Small generated environment texture optional, <=150 KiB.
- No huge photoreal HDRI downloads just to make metal look shiny.
- Keep art asset resolution independent from browser pixel ratio.

4.3 UI MICROINTERACTIONS
------------------------
Nav links: line grows from center 0->100%, 180ms.
CTA: background lightens 6%, shadow expands slightly, arrow moves +3px,
  160ms cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1).
Cards: border tint changes to G02, NOT glowing box-shadow everywhere.
Scroll prompt: small 10px vertical motion for 1 repeat, then stops.
Section reveal: opacity 0->1 and translateY 18->0px, 450-650ms;
  stagger 70ms; only once per viewport entry.
Scroll scene: scrub follows progress without scroll-jacking; never hijack
  browser wheel, keyboard PageDown, or scrollbar.

7.2 VISUAL STATES (DETERMINISTIC)
--------------------------------
IDLE / VOICE OFF:
  Flat neutral ring, outer diameter 28px, stroke #91A3BD opacity .7.
  Label 'Voice off' / 'Start voice'. Zero moving energy.
CONNECTING:
  Pale amber segmented ring rotates slowly MAX 1 turn / 2s only while real
  connection attempt is pending; label 'Connecting'. No fake microphone pulse.
LISTENING:
  Stable mint inner dot radius 3px + 1 constant thin mint border;
  label 'Listening'. There should be NO bouncing bars just because VAD is on.
WAITING / QUIET:
  Slate ring with soft center pearl; label 'Waiting'. No motion. This is
  important: meaningful deliberate silence is an intended experience.
SPEAKING:
  Outer ring color changes to cyan, inner segments grow according to real
  playback amplitude. Label 'Speaking'. ONLY when actual AI audio is audible
  or scheduled about to begin per reliable audio-clock event.
MUTED CANDIDATE MIC:
  Keep AI output state truthful; show separate 'Microphone muted' control.
  Do not conflate AI speaker status with local microphone status.
BARGE-IN:
  As soon as playback scheduler cancels/ducks confirmed interruption,
  energy collapses to neutral within 40-80ms, label becomes truthful live
  state; do not allow animation to finish a decorative 400ms flourish.
FAILED / DISCONNECTED:
  Ring stationary amber/coral as appropriate with explicit 'Connection lost'
  or 'Voice unavailable'; offer existing retry path.

7.3 SPEAKING ANIMATION CHOREOGRAPHY
-----------------------------------
T=0ms (playback actually starts):
  Rim shifts mint/neutral -> cyan in 120ms; center visible at 0.65 opacity.
T=60-180ms:
  3-5 amplitude bars rise; max heights 5, 9, 14, 9, 5px within 20px circle.
During real playback:
  Bars driven by envelope, with smoothing. Soft halo radius varies 12-20px.
  Only +/-3px amplitude change in border radius, <=1.025x total scale.
  No screen-wide waves, color explosion, editor shaking, or pulsating timer.
On natural drain:
  Falloff 160-240ms to the next truthful state. Stop animation loop.
On stop/barge-in/disconnect:
  Abort immediately and clear scheduled animation; no 'speaking' linger.

7.4 SIGNAL SOURCE — CRITICAL ENGINEERING DETAIL
-----------------------------------------------
The existing repository already has PlaybackScheduler in lib/playback.ts.
Its isPlaying flag is derived from scheduled sources and natural drain. It is
used by voice-session.ts to set VoiceStatus SPEAKING. DO NOT make the orb derive
speech from microphone VAD, raw transcript text, AI 'thinking', or timer ticks.

Preferred implementation:
 A. Start with EXISTING voiceStatus SPEAKING boolean for trustworthy visuals.
 B. Optionally add a LOCAL ephemeral playback-envelope hook in the browser's
    WebAudio OUTPUT chain (not the microphone input) for genuine audio-reactive
    amplitude. Signal path example:
      AI decoded PCM -> scheduled AudioBufferSources -> gain/output mix ->
      optional AnalyserNode tap -> speakers
    This exact insertion point must be verified against the voice-session
    AudioSink graph, including cached speech, speaker switching and ducking.
 C. Use AnalyserNode.getFloatTimeDomainData or getByteFrequencyData locally.
    Compute normalized RMS envelope; smooth it; map to bounded visual values.
 D. The analyzer arrays stay in memory only; never log or store audio data.
 E. No analyzer available? Use state-based, subtle preset 'speaking' motion,
    clearly a presence animation, not a fake actual waveform.
 F. If speech audio is authorized but playback stalls/suspends, the indicator
    must not pretend sound is being heard. Prefer actual playback lifecycle.

7.5 ENVELOPE MATH (IMPLEMENTATION STARTER)
------------------------------------------
Let x[i] be floating time-domain sample in [-1,1].
  rms = sqrt( sum(x[i]*x[i]) / N )
  normalized = clamp((rms - noiseFloor)/dynamicRange, 0, 1)
  target = smoothstep(0.08, 0.70, normalized)
  displayed = previous + (target - previous) * alpha
  attack ~60ms; release ~180ms; gain clamp prevents jitter.
  radius = 12px + 4px * displayed
  glowOpacity = .08 + .16 * displayed
  barHeight[j] = baseHeight[j] + envelope*scale[j]
Rendering ideally 30fps for small orb; stop the RAF when not speaking.
Analysis FFT ~256-512 is fine; never run large FFT in UI thread.
Do not promise exact speech-to-syllable alignment from simple RMS.

08 / MOTION SYSTEM — EVERY DURATION, PHYSICS AND RULE
===============================================================

MOTION CHARACTER: slow, weighted, premium, intentional. No bounces unless
feedback conveys actual physical interaction. UI keeps everything quick.

DESIGN TOKENS:
  instant       0ms
  micro         120ms
  quick         180ms
  normal        260ms
  reveal        520ms
  cinematic     900ms
  slow-idle     16000ms, landing hero only, pause when hidden

EASINGS:
  out-expo-lite = cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)      major scene reveal
  out-standard = cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)       controls/panels
  in-out-soft  = cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1)      symmetrical transition
  linear       = linear                         audio sampling/scroll scrub

MICROINTERACTION MATRIX:
  Nav hover:       180ms, line/opacity only.
  CTA hover:       160ms, shift arrow 3px + brightening.
  Modal enter:     220ms opacity + translateY 8->0px.
  Dashboard card:  180ms border + translateY -2px.
  Dashboard data:  no counting animation by default (avoid invented momentum).
  Code focus:      120ms border state only.
  Resizer:         direct response to pointer; NO easing / lag while dragging.
  Editor tabs:     120ms underline position/opacity.
  Save ack:        text change, optional 180ms check fade; no distracting toast.
  Speaking start:  120ms state shift, real amplitude reactive thereafter.
  Speaking stop:   immediate on interruption; 160-240ms on natural completion.
  Error/reconnect:  no shaking/strobe; static clear label.
  Timer tick:      text numeral only, tabular widths, never animate digit flips.

NO LOOPING ANIMATION UNLESS it is a real state indicator or an opted-in
marketing art element. The interview has no decorative perpetual loop.

SCROLL CONVENTIONS:
  Use GSAP ScrollTrigger on landing ONLY; pin one section at a time.
  Avoid scroll hijacking or making body scroll dependent on JS.
  One cinematic 3D scroll transition and 2-3 DOM reveals are sufficient.
  Use gsap.matchMedia to isolate desktop/reduced-motion effects and cleanup.
  On route change/unmount: kill ScrollTriggers and dispose scene resources.

REDUCED MOTION:
  CSS @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) disables 3D camera moves,
  parallax, decorative pulsing and long reveals. Essential state changes
  remain explicit, instantaneous labels.
  Offer Settings -> Visual effects = Full / Reduced / Off (optional addition).
  Defaults always respect OS. Manual choice must not override OS minimum
  accessibility expectation without explicit user action.

  mostly CSS/HTML, optional lightweight scene loaded after data and after
  initial interaction; default static fallback is intentional.

Interview:
  NO Three.js requirement. Monaco remains lazy/self-hosted.
  Speaking indicator uses SVG/CSS and, only if safe, Web Audio analyzer.
  No GSAP import into this route for simple state changes.

9.9 STATE & ASSET SEPARATION
----------------------------
Separate optional animation system from core product events:
  Core state       -> connection, playback, timer, saves, stage.
  Presentation     -> colors, shapes, GPU scene, motion preference.

Presentation may read core state. It may NEVER modify or delay core state.
No invalid state mapping; failures must appear as failures even with graphics
unavailable. Abort on React unmount; avoid stale status closures and leaking RAF.

9.10 GIT STRATEGY
-----------------
Create a feature branch, e.g. design/neural-chamber, only when implementation
is explicitly requested. This file does not authorize code writes or merges.
Commit by surface; no monolithic replacement of the voice system.
Prefer PRs with screenshots and tested motion alternatives.

  Evidence before estimates, explicit model limitations. No celebratory
  score bursts or impossible confidence visualization.

10.15 EmptyStatePanel
  Static hero shape, explanatory sentence, one helpful next action.
  Consistent for new account, zero reports, offline, missing session.

10.16 Settings / Appearance
  Dark / Light / System retained. Optional visual effects preference,
  no link between motion and consent. Keep privacy actions explicit.

11 / INTERACTIVE STATES & ERROR INVENTORY
===============================================================

By surface, implement all states with the same token system.

LANDING:
  server HTML, poster loaded, 3D downloading, 3D ready, WebGL error,
  slow network, reduced motion, mobile, no JS, authenticated/anonymous nav.

DASHBOARD:
  loading skeleton, empty first-use, active interview, recent completed,
  report pending, unknown aggregate, pagination, session unavailable,
  auth expired, network offline, server error, user deletion initiated.

INTERVIEW:
  starting voice, permission denied, connecting, listening, waiting,
  actual AI speaking, muted candidate mic, interruption, device changed,
  output sink unavailable, reconnecting, offline edits pending, saved,
  ending confirmation, final save in progress, report pending, ended.

REPORT:
  generating, ready, partial, unavailable, estimation disclaimer,
  privacy deletion, export failure.

EACH STATE MUST DEFINE:
  main label, 1-line helper, icon, color/token, motion behavior,
  keyboard affordance, accessibility announcement priority, recovery action.

STATE EXAMPLE (VOICE RECONNECTING):
  text: 'Reconnecting voice...'
  helper: 'Your code is still editable. Unsaved changes will retry.'
  color: amber; icon: connection/plug; pulse: none in reduced motion;
  action: Retry if existing flow allows; no provider-debug details shown.

STATE EXAMPLE (DATA UNAVAILABLE):
  value: '—'
  helper: 'Not enough completed sessions to show this.'
  icon: informational, not exclamation red; no fictitious mini chart.

12 / ACCESSIBILITY AND INPUT SAFETY CONTRACT
===============================================================

- WCAG 2.2 AA target. Use genuine semantic DOM headings/nav/main/sections.
- Single global status announcement channel (existing Preferences system).
- No more than one simultaneous aria-live announcement per meaningful state.
- No color-only status; every speaking/listening/error shows text and icon.
- Text contrast 4.5:1 normal / 3:1 large; interface affordances 3:1.
- 44px recommended pointer targets; minimum WCAG sizing rules observed.
- Focus visible on ALL controls and not hidden beneath sticky header/dialog.
- Test 200% / 400% zoom, portrait tablet, 360px/390px phones, Windows High
  Contrast and native OS reduced-motion.
- Reduced-motion static art communicates exactly the same information.
- Voice preflight remains explicit; no camera/webcam or auto-microphone usage.
- No audio playing automatically on landing, including ambient loops.
- Canvas aria-hidden=true if decorative; provide adjacent DOM labels/action.
- GPU-failure fallback must not consume keyboard focus or trap scrolling.
- Preserve candidate caption access, tab order, shortcuts and settings.
- Do not render full interview problems/transcripts to show off UI styling.
- Ensure report samples never resemble actual user's private evidence.
- Avoid loud audio-reactive screen flashes; bright transitions capped.

13 / PERFORMANCE, SECURITY AND RELIABILITY BUDGET
===============================================================

KNOWN PRIOR LOCAL UI CHECKPOINT (from docs/UI_REDESIGN_STATUS.md):
  Landing initial JS 108.6 KiB gzip, interview initial JS 129.7 KiB gzip,
  CSS 8.6 KiB gzip; local lab LCP ~517-831ms on landing in that report.
Those are historical local tests, not current live production measurements.

DESIRED NEW TARGETS (proposal; validate against real baselines):
  First visible landing HTML+text+poster available WITHOUT 3D JavaScript.
  No extra Three.js/GSAP bytes on interview route at all.
  New dashboard initial JS incremental addition <=45 KiB gzip if feasible.
  Landing art code split, not in initial critical path; compressed 3D assets
    <=500 KiB total for baseline scene, tune after visual A/B test.
  3D desktop target >=50fps on mainstream laptop; >=30fps limited devices.
  Input/coding interactions must remain crisp under scene transition.
  LCP <=2.5s at 75th percentile field, INP <=200ms, CLS <=0.1.
  Lazy art should not move text or buttons; reserve scene aspect ratio.
  Test page under 4x CPU throttle, low-end integrated graphics and WebGL off.

RUNTIME GUARDS:
  document.hidden -> stop decorative RAF and rendering loops.
  ResizeObserver/IntersectionObserver unsubscribed on unmount.
  context lost -> poster fallback; offer retry only if safe.
  route transition -> dispose materials, textures, events, audio analyzers.
  skip marketing scene when reduced motion or save-data is enabled.
  consider adaptive DPR and instancing rather than more full-screen shaders.

SECURITY/PRIVACY:
  - Do not weaken CSP to fetch random shaders/models from untrusted CDNs.
  - Self-host geometry, fonts and static art; audit asset licenses.
  - Do not embed private interview scripts, prompts or reference tests in
    client bundle or poster alt text.
  - Do not log PCM audio, envelope data, code revisions or transcript details
    in aesthetic telemetry. Anonymous frame-rate aggregates only if already
    allowed by privacy program.
  - Avoid extracting user info for decorative personalization.
  - Keep session APIs authenticated; changes to CSS/route UX cannot bypass it.
