Product direction
-----------------
Build a focused 35–45 minute interview cockpit, not a chatbot or a generic
LeetCode clone. The interviewer owns the audio channel, the candidate owns the
editor, and the interface should make connection, voice, time, and interview
stage legible without exposing the problem statement or the interviewer's
internal reasoning.

Visual direction
----------------
Use contemporary developer-tool and live-coding dashboard patterns as
inspiration: deep neutral surfaces, a strong editorial landing page, modular
workspace panels, restrained blue/coral accents, clear state pills, soft depth,
and short functional transitions. Preserve the product invariants: no written
problem, no interviewer transcript, no AI autocomplete, and no decorative
motion that creates extra pressure during the interview.
